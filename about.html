About Integrated Waste Consulting.

Integrated Waste Consulting has been helping companies save money on waste services since 2013. We are a family-owned company committed to high-quality waste management solutions that are both efficient and sustainable.

Est. 2013Booneville, MS

Who we are

Helping companies save since 2013.

Larry Nesler founded Integrated Waste Consulting in 2013 in Booneville, Mississippi. Our team of experts has years of experience in the industry and is dedicated to helping businesses of all sizes reduce their waste-related costs.

Today we manage waste services, audit invoices and rent compactors for LTL trucking terminals across the country, from our corporate office on Highway 4 West. North, south, east or west, we have you covered from coast to coast.
