What does a waste consultant do for a carrier?

We manage your waste services for you. We arrange and negotiate with the haulers in each market, audit every invoice and keep the reporting in one place.

Do you only work with LTL carriers?

LTL trucking terminals are our specialty, and we serve businesses of all sizes.

Can you cover terminals in different states?

Yes. North, south, east or west, we have you covered from coast to coast, from our office in Booneville, Mississippi.

Do we have to buy equipment?

No. Most clients rent, which includes delivery and maintenance. Equipment is also available to buy.
